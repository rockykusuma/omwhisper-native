Meetings grew up in this release, and the app now opens in light mode by default.

## The app looks different on first launch

OmWhisper now defaults to **Light**. If you never touched the appearance setting, the
window will look different than it did yesterday — that is this change, not something
broken. Settings › General has System / Light / Dark, and if you had already chosen one,
your choice is untouched.

## Meetings

Recorded calls used to give you a transcript and a summary. Now:

- **Real titles and attendees.** A meeting is named after the calendar event it matches,
  or the call window's title, instead of "zoom.us". Calendar matching is opt-in and reads
  nothing until you switch it on.
- **Name the speakers.** Click "Speaker 1" and rename it once — the name appears through
  the whole transcript, in copies, in exports, and in the summary after you regenerate.
  Attendees from the calendar show up as one-tap suggestions.
- **Summary templates.** Standard, Standup, Client call, 1:1, Interview, or write your own.
  Pick a default, or choose one for a single regeneration.
- **Edit anything.** Summaries are editable in place; titles and attendees can be typed by
  hand, which also works if you have no calendar connected at all.
- **Ask about a meeting.** One question, one answer, from that meeting's transcript. If it
  wasn't discussed, it says so rather than inventing something.
- **Export and follow-ups.** Save a meeting as Markdown or plain text, copy the summary, or
  draft a follow-up email from the notes.
- **Better notes on long calls.** Summaries of long meetings were quietly losing their
  second half. Fixed. If you use Ollama for AI cleanup, meeting summaries now go through it
  in much larger pieces, which reads noticeably better on an hour-long call.

All of it stays on your Mac. Meeting audio and text never reach a cloud provider, even if
you use a cloud engine for dictation — that is enforced in code, not by a setting.

## Fixes

- **Deleting a custom vocabulary word did nothing visible** until you navigated away and
  back. It was being deleted; the list just never redrew. This affected several settings
  that could change without the window noticing.
- Meeting summaries sometimes rendered as an empty card. The text was always saved — the
  app was failing to display it.
- The calendar permission prompt could never appear, because the app was missing the
  entitlement macOS requires to ask.
- Settings no longer claims the app is a "rewrite in progress".

## Under the hood

The first cross-engine accuracy benchmark now ships in the source tree, so engine
comparisons come from measurement rather than reputation. One finding worth stating plainly:
custom vocabulary has **no measurable effect** on Apple Speech or Parakeet — it does improve
Whisper. Word replacements and fuzzy correction work on every engine, because they run after
transcription rather than trying to bias it.
