# OmWhisper 2.0.10

Your microphone in meetings is now yours to control, AI features can each pick
their own backend, and several things that used to fail in silence now say so.

## Meetings: your own audio, on your terms

- **Record my microphone** is a setting, applied from the moment a call is
  detected — not just at the start of a recording. Turn it off and your voice is
  never written to disk at all; only the other side is captured.
- **Mute and discard mid-recording**, from the menu bar or the hub. Muting stops
  your mic being written from that moment; discarding drops what was already
  captured.
- **Delete my audio** on a meeting you have already recorded. It removes your
  track, drops your turns from the transcript, and rewrites the summary — so a
  search for something you said no longer finds it.
- Teams meetings are titled correctly. The meeting name lives in the *second*
  pipe segment of the window title, and Teams' own UI labels ("Chat", "Calls",
  "Calendar") are no longer mistaken for meeting names.

## An AI backend per feature

Dictation polish, Reply Assist, meeting summaries, chronicles and brain-dump each
choose their own backend. This exists because one setting could not express the
question that matters: **whether that feature's data leaves your Mac**. A day of
screen text, a recorded call with other people in it, and one dictated sentence
are not the same decision.

Cloud is only ever used where you explicitly asked for it.

## Onboarding offers local AI

A new, skippable step offers the two **on-device** backends — Apple Intelligence
and Ollama. Cloud is deliberately absent from first-run: it needs an API key,
costs money, and sends text off your Mac.

If Ollama isn't set up, the step now says which of the four things is actually
true — not installed, installed but not running, running with no models, or
ready — instead of asking whether a service you never installed is running.

## Things that used to fail quietly

- **A failed polish now tells you.** When the backend is unreachable or
  misconfigured, Polish Selected Text and Smart Dictation showed nothing and
  pasted your text back unchanged, which is indistinguishable from working.
- **Brain-dump reports a failed structuring** instead of pasting the raw ramble.
- **Global shortcuts no longer leak into the app underneath.** ⌘⇧P is Page Setup
  and ⌘⇧\ is Center in most document apps, so the shortcut ran a menu command in
  your document every time you used it. The default for Polish Selected Text is
  now **⌘⇧\**, and Smart Dictation stays ⌘⇧B.
- **The app no longer freezes reading its own accessibility tree**, and every
  accessibility read is bounded so an unresponsive app cannot wedge a thread.

## Faster, quieter memory

Passages are indexed by content hash, so unchanged text is never embedded twice,
and the indexing backlog drains across ticks instead of in one burst.
